Turbine seals, instrumentation, and assembly.

Designed F135 turbine seals and supporting hardware from preliminary through detailed design.

RoleMilitary Turbine Design Engineer · Co-op

01

Component ownership

Designed second- and third-stage blade outer air seals (BOAS), with supporting work on the third-stage brush seal and retention hardware. Advanced designs from preliminary to detailed design within an integrated team at North Berwick, working with Structures, Durability, and Aerothermal.

02

Design and validation

  • Test-engine instrumentation: Integrated blade-tip-clearance capacitance probes into BOAS variants. Modified probe interfaces, restored cooling circuits with EDM inlets/outlets, routed wires, and used printed models to check assembly.
  • Assembly risk: Evaluated 10 high-risk brush-seal misassembly configurations with Aerothermal. Used decision matrices to weigh machinability, cost, and schedule.
  • Fits and thermal behavior: Performed GD&T tolerance stacks and hand calculations for fits and clearances. Worked with specialist teams on thermal deflection, wear, cracking, and transient conditions.
  • CAD and documentation: Refined NX models, cross-sections, and BOMs; supported material and specification decisions.

Technical details are limited by confidentiality.
