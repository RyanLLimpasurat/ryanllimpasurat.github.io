Robotics

A hopping robot and rotary test fixture.

Built a 580 g hopper and rotary test boom; integrated power, motors, and sensors.

01

Project scope

BAR Hopper pairs a bicopter with a spring-loaded leg to explore hopping and ground-to-air motion. I owned mechanical and electrical hardware; Shazaib Lalani developed software and controls.

Watch project videos

02

Hopper and test fixture

Built the 580 g hopper from carbon-fiber arms, PETG parts, and aluminum tubes. Designed a rotary boom using birch spars and printed parts to constrain motion to a vertical plane during testing.

03

Power and sensing

Integrated a 3S LiPo, power distribution, two motor/ESC channels, and 5 V / 3.3 V supplies. Instrumented the robot with an IMU, time-of-flight height sensing, and a magnetic encoder linked to leg compression.

04

Team testing

Used the boom for hopping and ground-contact experiments. Sensor noise and thrust saturation limited testing performance.
