01

Project scope

DAVE is a passive Rogallo-wing glider designed to deploy from a 6-inch rocket. The flexible wing packages compactly for launch and supports an unpowered descent.

02

Design and flight testing

  • Design iteration: Led CAD development, coordinated printed prototypes for ejection tests, and directed aerostructure changes for the next glider iteration.
  • Team coordination: Coordinated an eight-member team across design, testing, and external communications.
  • Flight trials: The team conducted two high-altitude and six low-altitude tests to investigate passive turning, extended flight, and potential stall behavior.
