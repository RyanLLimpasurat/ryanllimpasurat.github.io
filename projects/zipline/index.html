01

My scope

Mechanical design co-op on the Droid Structures, Mechanisms, and Integration team in South San Francisco. My work covered launcher hardware, production testing, component design, and failure investigations.

02

High-speed launcher

Designed and analyzed a 33 m, dual-motor rail launcher for parachute testing of a 28 kg vehicle. Used hand calculations and FEA, coordinated approximately $60K in materials, and supported procurement and on-site assembly over two months.

03

Production lid tester

Designed a pneumatic, sensor-integrated tester to proof-load lid welds to 445 N. Integrated automated data processing and failure classification. The production workflow tested approximately 600 lids at roughly 25 s per cycle.

04

Failure investigation

Designed and modified fixtures for 25+ pneumatic and Mark-10 load tests. Force–displacement data and failure inspections identified incorrect heat-set inserts and misoriented mold-in inserts. Wider weld joints reached failure loads up to 1.6 kN.

05

Injection-molded hooks

Designed a molded alternative to CNC-machined aluminum hooks. Used FEA to refine geometry and mold-flow analysis to assess manufacturability. The revised design eliminated adhesive requirements.

06

Electrical contact testing

Built Teensy 4.1 breadboard circuits to detect conductive paths and contact interruptions during vibration testing, supporting early validation of the next hardware iteration.

07

Wind-tunnel retrofit parts

Created FDM parts from existing outer mold line geometry, adding extended surfaces and modular interfaces for quick configuration changes. The resulting wind-tunnel tests informed the next design iteration.
