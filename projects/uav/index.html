My contributions

  • Central hub: Prepared carbon-fiber waterjet geometry and contributed to a modular frame layout that increased frame area by 15%.
  • Landing gear: Developed printed leg mounts using ribs and tension-fit features.
  • Battery mounts: Designed four mounts to accommodate 2 kg LiPo batteries and also support the structure; iterated through five printed prototypes.
  • Avionics: Designed printed enclosures, including a radio-receiver housing.
  • Field testing: Assisted with assembly, maintenance, and troubleshooting during three field tests.
