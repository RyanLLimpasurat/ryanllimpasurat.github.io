Integrating a wheel chassis with a quadrotor.

01

Project scope

TWISTR combines wheel-driven motion with quadrotor thrust. I owned mechanical and electrical hardware; two EECS teammates developed software and controls.

Watch project videos

02

Mechanical integration

Designed the mechanical assembly in Siemens NX around a retrofitted carbon-fiber drone frame. PETG chassis parts, M3 joints, and separate internal and external structures supported electronics mounting, repairability, and the drone-to-wheel connection.

03

Electrical integration

Integrated a 3S LiPo, power distribution board, quadrotor ESCs, and wheel-motor drivers. Connected encoders, an IMU, and a time-of-flight sensor with an ESP32 and Naze32 flight controller.

04

Team testing and rebuild

Drop tests exposed internal-frame failures and informed the next iteration. During later flight trials in a netted enclosure, a seven-foot fall damaged the internal structure and required an overnight rebuild.
